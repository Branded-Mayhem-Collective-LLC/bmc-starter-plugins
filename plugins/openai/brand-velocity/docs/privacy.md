# Privacy candidate v1 — review before public hosting
No user account or email is required. Host conversations follow the selected host's own privacy policy. This starter service processes workflow descriptions, permitted supplied source text and results only in request memory; it does not durably save them or send them to a separate model API. UI edits remain in view memory until copied, exported or explicitly shared with the conversation.

Website checks send the selected public website URLs to their respective sites. Selected public search phrases, market and fixed search options are sent to DataForSEO. Do not supply confidential queries or personal information. The service caches public website and SERP evidence up to 24 hours and keyword estimates up to 30 days. Disclosed technical data: opaque request hashes, UTC monthly spend reservations, timestamps/status and minute-level route counters. Spend reservations expire after 90 days; minute counters after a day. Expired rows are unavailable immediately and physically removed on the next service request; an idle service retains expired rows until it next receives a request. No workflow answers, emails or user/IP identities are stored in that ledger.

Provider credentials stay server-side under Doppler. Production proxy and service logging must exclude request bodies, authorization headers, page bodies and provider error payloads. Network providers may retain their own technical logs; their terms and final hosting policy must be reviewed at activation. No durable user profile, advertising pixel or automatic contact record is created. Arrivals at a brand website may use starter/platform UTM attribution only, never answers or personal data. Enquiries use the website's disclosed existing systems.

Support candidate: hello@brandedmayhem.com. Release owner must verify the receiving route and publish this accepted policy before submission. This draft is accurate for candidate source; production retention and host behavior require separate verification.

Public-data caches are additionally bounded to 2,500 entries and 32 MiB of serialized text; single entries above 512 KiB are discarded and older public entries may be evicted before their TTL. No new expiry scheduler is installed.
